Privacy Policy — Block Bloom! Puzzle

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Block Bloom! Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") is an offline block-puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal data. The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal identifiers. Your game progress, scores, and best scores are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any information, there is nothing to use, sell, or share. Everything stays on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. The current version of the app uses Google's official test ad units only; no real advertising identifiers are monetized in this version.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no account data for us to show, correct, or delete. You can reset all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
